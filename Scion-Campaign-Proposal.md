### What's the justification for restructuring the Scion campaign?
The current Scion campaign in stock is riddled with plot holes and rushed ideas, making it feel more like a time-crunched project rather than a well-thought-out set of missions.

The proposal is to take the existing story in the current stock campaign and restructure the missions to better tie everything together while addressing the issues that make the narrative feel disjointed.

### Proposal
Remaking the campaign will require significant effort to ensure all the pieces fit together seamlessly from both a designer's and a player's perspective. That said, leaving the campaign as is would feel like a missed opportunity to improve the player experience.

Regarding the missions, I believe the order can be adjusted to make the experience more engaging and enjoyable.
### 1. Replace Transformation with Crystals
The first Scion mission offers little in terms of playability. The player is tasked with completing some very basic objectives before being ordered to escort a Power Crystal to a dropship, which was somehow obtained by the Scions without much resistance from Braddock. The original designs stated that Braddock had hidden the crystals in various locations on Mire. While this is not inherently problematic, it still doesn’t make sense why Braddock would allow two Scion scouts to obtain the crystal and return to the location where Yelena is teaching Cooke about the Scion tech tree.

Therefore, I propose scrapping the first mission and replacing it with the third mission, where the player and Yelena must assault an ISDF base on Mire to regain the first Power Crystal.

We can easily add a cutscene at the beginning, showing Yelena and Cooke moving into a Kiln, with Cooke’s transformation starting.

Once the transformation sequence is complete, Yelena will order the two scouts to search for the ISDF base, as originally planned in the "Crystals" mission.

The mission will then progress with the player tasked to build up their base and forces, before assaulting the ISDF base to retrieve the first Power Crystal.
### 2. Ambush
This mission has already been heavily rewritten in BZCC:R. Some minor tweaks could still be made, but it seems suitable enough to be part of the campaign.

Once Yelena and Cooke have obtained the second Power Crystal from the ISDF base, they move toward the Mire Alchemator under the cover of a Jammer to prevent Braddock’s forces from discovering that they possess the crystal.

John and Yelena must neutralize the ISDF defenses in the area and proceed to activate the Mire Alchemator with the first Power Crystal.
### 3. Merge "An Unlikely Rescue" and "The AAN"
After the loss of the first Power Crystal on Mire, Braddock orders the relocation of the remaining two crystals—one to Bane and another to Rend.

Not all members of the Scion Convergence agree with Frank’s plan and have broken off to rebel against him. Unbeknownst to the Convergence, the Scion rebels have formed a transactional pact with Braddock. They are tasked with transporting one of the crystals to Bane to meet with New Regime forces.

The ISDF is also facing its own division. Braddock has formed the New Regime, but not all of his men have pledged their loyalty. Manson, who survived the Scion attack on Rend, has broken off to lead a faction of AAN Loyalists.

Manson and his band of loyalists are under threat from New Regime forces on Bane, and without assistance, they will soon fall. Braddock is confident Manson will be defeated soon, and has ordered the rebel Scions to deliver the crystal to his base on Bane once Manson has been defeated.

Scion strategists believe that if Burns and the Scions ally with Manson and the AAN, their combined forces could overthrow the New Regime.

John and Yelena travel to Bane to establish a base in the AAN sector, where New Regime forces are attacking Manson.

The first part of the mission involves staving off Braddock’s men and supporting the AAN in rebuilding their base. This will require slowing down the New Regime by any means necessary and aiding Manson’s forces before they are defeated.

Once the AAN and Scion forces establish enough of a presence, they will both attack the New Regime base.

After the New Regime base is defeated, the rebel Scion convoy will approach, where Manson and the AAN forces will intercept and secure the crystal. Manson will hand over the crystal to Yelena and John and express his gratitude.

With the second crystal secured, Yelena and John will proceed to the Alchemator on Bane to activate it.
### 4. New Mission: Activate the Bane Alchemator.
Braddock is slowly losing control. His desperation is becoming more apparent by the day. He has ordered his men to establish a base around the Bane Alchemator and secure it to prevent John and Yelena from activating the machine.

Yelena and John (now a Royal Scion) must establish a base and attack the New Regime. Once victorious, they will transport the crystal to the Alchemator and begin the activation process.
### 5. Braddock
With the Bane and Mire Alchemators prepared, there is one final step to ensure the Scion plan succeeds: activating the final Alchemator.

The New Regime has suffered heavy losses, and Braddock is desperate. He has taken the third Power Crystal to Rend and established a fortress around the final Alchemator, where he is holding the crystal.

Yelena and John travel to Rend for the final battle against the New Regime. Upon arrival, it’s clear that Braddock has prepared. His troops have captured all available resource points in a desperate attempt to starve the Scions of scrap, preventing them from building a large enough force to overrun the Alchemator.

After the AAN rescue, Burns invites the AAN to travel to Sol once the Dark Planet has been terraformed into the new "Dark-Core." Manson agrees, pledging volunteers to assist with the assault on Braddock and the New Regime.

Yelena and John must establish a base, slowly wrest control from Braddock, and start collecting enough resources to build their army. AAN volunteers will periodically be available throughout the mission.

The final battle continues. Braddock must be defeated, the crystal captured, and the final Alchemator activated.

If successful, the Scion plan for "Dark-Core" will be set in motion, allowing for the creation of a new Core planet in the Sol system.
