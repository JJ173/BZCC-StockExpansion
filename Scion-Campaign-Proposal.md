### What's the justification for restructuring the Scion campaign?
The current Scion campaign in Stock is full of plot holes and rushed ideas which makes it feel more like a time-crunched project rather than an actual well thought-out set of missions.

The proposal is to take the existing story included in the current Stock campaign, and restructure the missions to make it all tie together a bit more, and make try and remove the obvious issues that make you question the point of it.

### Proposal
Remaking the campaign is going to be a lot of effort to ensure all pieces of the puzzle fit, and that it makes sense from both a designer, and player point of view. That being said, leaving the campaign in its current state feels like the mod would be letting the player base down if nothing is done to fix it.

Regarding the missions, I believe the order can be changed to make the experience less boring, and more enjoyable. 
### 1. Replace Transformation with Crystals
The first Scion mission offers very little in terms of playability. The player is tasked with some very basic objectives, before being ordered to escort a Power Crystal to a Dropship that was somehow obtained by the Scions without much resistance from Braddock. The original designs claimed that Braddock had hidden the Crystals in various locations on Mire. Whilst this in itself is not a problem, it still doesn't make much sense as to why Braddock would allow two Scion scouts to obtain the Crystal and just head back to the location where Yelena is teaching Cooke about the Scion tech tree.

Therefore, I propose that the first mission is scrapped, and replaced with the third mission, where the Player and Yelena have to assault an ISDF base in a sector of Mire to regain the first Power Crystal.

We can quite easily add the cutscene to the beginning of that, where Yelena and Cooke move into a Kiln, and Cooke's transformation begins.

Once the transformation sequence has been completed, Yelena will order the two Scouts to search for the ISDF base as originally done in the Crystals mission.

The main portion of the mission can then begin where the player is tasked to build up their base and forces, and assault the ISDF base to obtain the first Power Crystal.
### 2. Ambush
This mission has already been heavily re-written in BZCC:R. There are some tweaks that could be made but this still seems suitable enough to be part of the Campaign.

Once Yelena and Cooke have obtained the second Power Crystal from the ISDF base. They move to the Mire Alchemator under the cover of a Jammer to prevent Braddock's forces from discovering that they are in possession of the Crystal.

John and Yelena must neutralize the ISDF defences in the area, and proceed to activate the Mire Alchemator with the first Power Crystal.
### 3. Merge "An Unlikely Rescue" and "The AAN"
After the loss of the first Power Crystal on Mire. Braddock has ordered the relocation of the remaining two Crystals, one to be transported to Bane, and another to Rend. 

Not all members of the The Scion Convergence agree to Frank's plan, and have broken off to rebel against him. Undenounced to the Convergence, the Scion rebels have formed a transactional pact with Braddock. They have been tasked with transporting one of the Crystals to Bane to meet with New Regime forces.

The ISDF is facing its own split.  Braddock has formed the New Regime but not all of his men have pledged their loyalty.  Manson, who survived the Scion attack on Rend, has broken off to lead a faction of AAN Loyalists.

Manson and his band of loyalists are currently under threat by New Regime forces on Bane, and without assistance, they will soon fall.  Braddock is confident that Manson will soon fall, and has ordered the rebel Scions to deliver the Crystal to his base in that sector once Manson has been defeated.

Scion strategists theorize that if Burns and the Scions ally with Manson and the AAN, the two forces together could overthrow the New Regime.

John and Yelena travel to Bane to establish a base in the AAN sector, where the New Regime forces are attacking Manson. 

The first portion of the mission is to stave off Braddock's men, and support the AAN with rebuilding their base. This means slowing down the New Regime by any means necessary, and aiding Manson and his men before they are defeated.

Once the AAN and Scion forces establish enough of a presence, they both will attack the New Regime base.

Upon defeat of the New Regime base. The rebel Scion convoy will approach, where Manson and the AAN forces will intercept and secure the Crystal. Manson will handover the Crystal to Yelena and John, and express his gratitude. 

With the Second Crystal secured, Yelena and John will proceed to the Alchemator on Bane to activate it.
### 4. New Mission: Activate the Bane Alchemator.
Braddock is slowly losing control. His desperation becoming more obvious by the day. He has ordered that his men establish a base around the Bane Alchemator, and secure it to prevent John and Yelena from activating the machine. 

Yelena and John (now a Royal Scion) must establish a base, and attack the New Regime. Once victorious, they are to transport the Crystal to the Alchemator and begin the activation process.
### 5. Braddock
With the Bane and Mire Alchemators now prepared, there is one final step that must be done before the Scion plan is to succeed; the activation of the final Alchemator. 

The New Regime has suffered heavy losses. Braddock is desperate. He has taken the third Power Crystal to Rend, and established a Fortress around the final Alchemator, and is holding the final Power Crystal there.

Yelena and John travel to Rend, to battle the new Regime one final time. Upon arrival, it is obvious that Braddock has prepared. His troops have captured all available resource points in a desperate attempt to starve the Scion's of scrap, to stop them from establishing a force large enough to overrun the Alchemator.

After the AAN rescue, Burns has invited the AAN to travel to Sol, once the Dark Planet has been terraformed to the new "Dark-Core". Manson agrees, pledging volunteers to assist with the assault on Braddock and the New Regime where possible.

Yelena and John must establish a base, slowly wrestle control away from Braddock, and start collecting enough resources to build up their army. AAN volunteers will be periodically available throughout the mission.

The final battle continues. Braddock must be defeated, the Crystal captured, and the final Alchemator activated.

If successful, the Scion plan for "Dark-Core" will be set in motion, allowing the creation of a new Core planet in the Sol system.
